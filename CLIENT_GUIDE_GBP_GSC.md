# PART 2: Google Search Console (GSC)

### What is it?
A free dashboard showing: which searches bring people to bombaydar.com, how many clicks you get, and any website problems. We use it heavily for SEO work.

---

### Step 1 — Open Search Console
Go to: **search.google.com/search-console** → sign in with the same Google account.

---

### Step 2 — Add Your Website
1. Choose **"+ Add Property"** (top left dropdown)
2. Select **"Domain"** (left option) — covers the whole website including all pages
3. Enter: `bombaydar.com` → Continue

---

### Step 3 — Verify Ownership
Google shows a line of code called a **TXT record**. This sounds scary but it's just copying-pasting one line into your domain settings.

**Where is your domain managed?** Check where bombaydar.com was purchased:
- If through **Vercel** (where the website lives) → open vercel.com → your domain → DNS records → paste the TXT record there
- If through another company (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.) → log in there → find "DNS settings" or "DNS records" → add a TXT record with what Google showed you

Then click **Verify** in Google. ✅ Done — takes up to 48h sometimes.

> 💡 If this feels confusing: just tell us **where you bought the domain**, and we'll walk you through it on a call. Everything else above stays yours.

---

2. Click **Add user**

4. Permission level: **Full** → Add
