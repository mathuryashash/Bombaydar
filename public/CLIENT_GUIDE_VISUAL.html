3

Verify ownership — one copy-paste

Google shows one line of text called a TXT record. You just paste it into your domain settings:

google-site-verification=XyZ123abc... ← copy this line Google shows you
🔒 your domain provider — DNS records
DNS Records → Add record
TypeNameValue
TXT ✅ @ google-site-verification=XyZ123abc...
  1. Open the company where bombaydar.com was bought (Vercel, GoDaddy, Namecheap…)
  2. Find “DNS settings” or “DNS records”
  3. Add a TXT record → paste Google's line → Save
  4. Back in Google, click Verify ✅ (can take up to 48h)
💡
If DNS feels confusing: just tell us where you bought the domain and we'll walk you through it on a call.
