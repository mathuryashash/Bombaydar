Week 1 — Critical Fixes

Technical Foundation

Seven blocking issues preventing indexation and rich results. All implemented via feature/seo-foundation branch with Vercel preview.

01 P0

XML Sitemap Missing

Google cannot efficiently discover all pages — no /sitemap.xml exists.

Add next-sitemap config; auto-generates on build.

02 P0

No robots.txt

Crawlers lack guidance on what to index and where sitemap lives.

Add public/robots.txt with sitemap reference.

03 P0

Zero Schema Markup

No JSON-LD for Restaurant, LocalBusiness, Menu — missing stars, hours, menu in SERP.

Implement structured data on all pages (Organization, 3×LocalBusiness, Menu, MenuItem).

04 P0

/locations Redirects to Home

Kills internal link equity to 3 branch pages — they're orphaned.

Replace redirect with proper index page linking Gueliz, Medina, Casablanca.

05 P0

No Unique Meta per Page

All pages share generic title/description — low CTR from search results.

Add page-specific metadata export with optimized titles (≤60 chars) & descriptions (150-160 chars).

06 P1

No hreflang / Language URLs

French content invisible to French searchers — language switch is client-side only.

Implement /en/ & /fr/ subdirectories or hreflang annotations.

07 P0

Google Business Profiles Unclaimed

Zero map pack visibility for "indian restaurant near me" in Marrakech/Casablanca.

Claim & optimize 3 profiles: Gueliz, Medina Rooftop, Casablanca Maârif.
